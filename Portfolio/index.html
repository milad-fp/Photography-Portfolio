About Me

I'm Alex Morgan, a fashion and editorial photographer drawn to quiet moments, strong silhouettes, and honest expressions. My work explores the space between simplicity and emotion, creating images that feel timeless, intimate, and carefully considered.

From portraits and fashion campaigns to editorial stories, I focus on creating photographs with a distinct visual identity — balancing natural light, composition, and mood.

Based in London, available worldwide.
